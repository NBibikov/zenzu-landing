## Where LingQ is the better choice

- **Language coverage.** With more than 50 languages, LingQ covers far more than Zenzu's four.
- **A ready library.** You can start reading and listening immediately without finding your own material.
- **Reading-first immersion.** If your main goal is extensive reading and listening, LingQ's lesson view and word-status tracking are designed for exactly that.
- **Android.** LingQ has a native Android app; Zenzu on Android is not publicly available yet.

## Where Zenzu is the better choice

- **Retention is the core, not an add-on.** Every saved word or phrase goes into an FSRS-5 schedule, so the vocabulary you meet in content is actually reviewed at the right moment.
- **Phrases, not just words.** Select a whole phrase and Zenzu saves it with cloze deletion and its source sentence.
- **Explanations in your native language.** Lisa, the AI coach, and the whole interface work in English, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese.

## Bottom line

LingQ is the broader platform for input-heavy learning across many languages. Zenzu is the sharper tool if you learn one of its four languages and want what you read and watch to stick through spaced repetition.
