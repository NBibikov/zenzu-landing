## Where Language Reactor is the better choice

- **Netflix.** Zenzu does not work with Netflix; Language Reactor was built for it.
- **Watching in place.** The extension sits on top of the streaming site, so you never leave the player.
- **Sentence-mining into Anki.** If you already run an Anki workflow, Pro's export slots straight into it.

## Where Zenzu is the better choice

- **Mobile.** Language Reactor lives in a desktop browser; Zenzu runs on iPhone and iPad as well as the web.
- **The review loop is included.** Saved words and phrases are scheduled with FSRS-5 inside the same app — no export step, no second tool to maintain.
- **Beyond video.** Podcasts are transcribed so you can read along and save phrases, and articles or photos of text become vocabulary too.
- **Explanations in your language.** Lisa and the interface speak English, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese.

## Bottom line

Language Reactor is an excellent subtitle layer for desktop viewing. Zenzu is a complete capture-and-review loop you can carry on your phone.
