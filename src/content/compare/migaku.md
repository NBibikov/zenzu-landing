## Where Migaku is the better choice

- **East Asian languages.** Migaku supports Japanese, Mandarin, Korean and Cantonese, with structured beginner courses for the first three. Zenzu doesn't teach these languages.
- **Streaming coverage.** Its browser extension works on web pages and several streaming sites, not only YouTube.
- **Anki compatibility.** You can bring existing Anki decks into Migaku.

## Where Zenzu is the better choice

- **Podcasts.** Episodes are transcribed so you can read along and save any phrase with pronunciation.
- **Structured learning for European languages.** AI courses cover grammar and vocabulary from A1 to C1 in English, German, French and Spanish, next to 18 curated starter decks.
- **Explanations in your language.** Lisa and the interface work in English, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese.

## Bottom line

Both apps are built around the same belief — learn from content you care about and let spaced repetition do the remembering. Pick Migaku for East Asian languages and streaming breadth, Zenzu for English, German, French and Spanish.
